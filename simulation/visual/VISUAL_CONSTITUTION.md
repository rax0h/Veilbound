# Veilbound Visual Constitution v1.0 — LOCKED

This is a production contract, not a mood board.

## Authority
The files in `visual/references/` are the visual source of truth. `reference-zero.png` is the north star. The broader boards define asset families, world breadth, UI language, creature treatment, character treatment, manifestations, equipment, settlements, and gameplay presentation.

## Prime rule
**A technically functional asset that violates Veilbound's visual language is a failed asset.**

## Camera and composition
- Elevated, forward-looking three-quarter gameplay camera.
- Perspective-rich world presentation; never a flat orthographic/mobile-board look.
- Player occupies roughly 10–14% of screen height during standard exploration.
- The world dominates the frame. Foreground, midground and distant world must all read.
- Deep sightlines, atmospheric perspective, terrain height, architecture and natural framing are expected.

## Surface and atmosphere
- Premium illustrated fantasy realism / stylized realism.
- Painterly but materially credible stone, timber, metal, cloth, soil, water, bark, foliage, fur and skin.
- Volumetric or painterly light shafts, depth haze, local fog, weathering and tonal separation.
- Beauty comes before darkness. Veilbound may be dangerous, ancient and uncanny without becoming uniformly muddy or black.

## Characters
Each production character family must have distinct assets for:
1. exploration body,
2. battle body/representation,
3. UI/dialogue portrait.

Do not stretch portraits into sprites. Do not reuse one image across all presentation classes. Gameplay silhouettes must remain readable against detailed environments.

## Creatures
Creatures are grounded enough to feel alive: coherent anatomy, specific fur/scale/chitin/skin, readable silhouettes, believable weight, and coloration integrated with their biome.

## Magic and manifestations
Magic may be spectacular, but it is physically situated in the scene. Emissive effects light nearby surfaces and respect depth. Avoid generic neon overlays. Essence identity comes from material behavior and semantic identity, not merely hue.

## UI
Restrained ornate fantasy. Dark translucent/material-backed surfaces, thin gold or bronze detail, elegant serif display language, high legibility, and maximum preservation of the world view. Mobile controls are minimal overlays, not slabs.

## Automatic rejection
Flat vector villages, primitive shapes passed off as final environments, orthographic board-game framing, chibi/toy proportions, generic mobile-game art, single-plane backgrounds, huge opaque black HUD boxes, portrait-as-sprite shortcuts, untextured production geometry, or visual assets with no explicit lineage to the locked references.

## Production gate
Every asset manifest must name the reference files that control it and declare its class, camera assumptions where relevant, final/placeholder status, and character asset separation. The verifier rejects violations before an asset can be marked production-ready.
