# v7.6 Persistent Expedition Economy

14,400 complete expeditions across 16 runs.
Every accepted expedition traversed:
intent → intelligence → party → preparation → travel → discovery → decision → return → consequence.

Results:
- 10,506 successful outcomes
- 3,894 failed outcomes
- 493 surge operations
- 21,230 persistent mission-created/recovered records or objects
- zero hard failures

Outputs included 5,694 monster remains,
2,686 cores,
741 Essences,
529 Awakening Stones,
and 5,295 harvested materials.

The first integrated balance exposed two composition errors: zero-required supply categories incorrectly reduced preparation quality, and the challenge floor made ordinary low-rank work implausibly punishing. Both were corrected structurally rather than by adding survival clamps.

Mission outputs now have persistent identity, origin, title and history. They are not anonymous loot-table counters.

Next gate: transport, processing, sale/use/inheritance and market circulation of these persistent goods.
