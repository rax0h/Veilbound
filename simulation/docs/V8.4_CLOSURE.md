Acceptance suite: 8 independent 1,000-year worlds.

Results:
- New-money/business-created dynasties appeared in 8/8 worlds: [3, 3, 6, 9, 1, 5, 4, 2]
- Pre-existing old houses survived as dynasties in 8/8 worlds: [3, 4, 9, 6, 3, 5, 8, 1]
- Once-wealthy families later collapsed in 7/8 worlds: [2, 3, 1, 1, 2, 1, 1, 0]
- Longest surviving enterprise: 1000 years
- Highest family magical rank observed in this small economic suite: 3
- Wealth remained bounded in the accepted calibration (maximum 45,909.2); an earlier exponential-capital calibration was explicitly rejected.

Causal rules:
1. Business formation requires capital/opportunity; success depends on quality, management, demand, costs, debt, shocks, reputation and reinvestment.
2. Profits can accumulate into property, records, prestige and future opportunity.
3. Dynasty continuity requires lineage attachment plus actual institutions/assets. It is not granted by wealth.
4. Wealth/rank increase the family's capability to preserve continuity, never obedience or guaranteed survival.
5. Magic access may become more available through wealth/prestige networks, but rank advancement is not purchasable and never mission-count/calendar automatic.
6. Succession can fragment property/name continuity; biological extinction remains possible.
7. Old wealth can survive for a millennium, new wealth can become dynastic, and either can collapse.

Example causal new-money line (family 60, seed 843000): first business Year 5 → estate Year 68 → repeated business formation/failure → magical entry Year 275 → 30 generations → 250+ accumulated dynasty years by Year 1000. This family began as an ordinary Year-0 family.
