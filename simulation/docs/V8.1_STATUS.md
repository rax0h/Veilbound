# v8.1 — Household Demography Integration

Not closed.

The first explicit household/partner/reproductive-intent integration collapsed. Audits found underpowered conception hazards, an under-replacement family-preference distribution, and weak adaptive matchmaking. Those were corrected structurally.

Current 4×1000 validation reaches year 1000 in all four worlds with zero hard accounting failures, but populations decline to 193, 287, 32 and 12. Mean completed children per woman is ~1.72. This is not acceptable closure.

No population floor, fertility rescue, or target-based feedback was added. The remaining task is causal family completion: partner access, timing, care/prosperity, spacing and intent must permit heterogeneous desired families to actually occur often enough.
